Drop Details

What you should know before ordering

Fabric

Heavyweight 80/20 cotton-poly fleece on the crewneck. Soft ring-spun 100% cotton on the tee. Both pieces run true to size with a relaxed, modern fit.

Print

Front-chest print using a fine-detail DTG process on the tee and discharge ink screen print on the sweatshirt, both designed to keep the original brushwork visible.

Shipping

Made-to-order by the studio. Orders ship within 7–10 business days from the United States via tracked Standard or Priority mail.

Care

Machine wash cold inside-out with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. Iron in reverse, avoiding the printed area. Do not bleach.

This is a limited studio drop. If your size sells out, please send a note and we will let you know if a restock is planned.
