Artist Statement

I am a mixed-media artist working primarily in painting and sculptural media. My practice investigates the unstable boundaries between material and immaterial experience, exploring duality, spectrality, and the continual transformation of matter through time. I am interested in the ways objects record memory, decay, and touch, and how entropy becomes both a physical process and a metaphor for identity, perception, and belief.

My work considers material as something alive rather than fixed, but constantly shifting through cycles of construction, deterioration, and renewal. By juxtaposing contrasting forms, surfaces, and symbols, I create spaces where oppositions such as presence and absence, body and spirit, permanence and ephemerality, coexist rather than resolve.

Drawing from dreams, personal mythology, and symbolic language alongside broader philosophical and spiritual traditions, I approach making as a process of excavation. I am interested in dreams not simply as private experiences, but as sites where memory, desire, fear, and archetype converge, blurring distinctions between the psychic and the material.
